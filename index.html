羽球配對 🏸

給羽球球隊用的排場小幫手：球友用邀請碼加入球隊、按一下報名，主辦者報到後一鍵排出程度相近的雙打配對，打完任何一位球員按勝負，級數會依戰績自動調整。

iPhone 專用，資料同步在雲端，所有管理員看到的都是同一份。

Badminton Pairing

A pairing helper for badminton clubs: members join with an invite code and tap to sign up, the organizer checks people in and generates balanced doubles matches in one tap, any player on court reports the winner, and levels adjust automatically from results.

iPhone only. Data syncs in the cloud so every admin sees the same thing.
